About Lions Club

Lions Clubs International is the world's largest service club organization, with over 1.4 million members in more than 46,000 clubs worldwide. We're dedicated to serving communities and helping those in need through various charitable initiatives.

All proceeds from this ice cream event will go directly to supporting local community projects, including vision care programs, youth education, and environmental initiatives.
